Physics & Optics Research

Seeing Sound with Light

Undergraduate Research · Sound, Made Visible

Led a 10-week research project (Summer-Tehqiq) to make sound into something you can look at. A balloon is stretched over a speaker with a small mirror glued to its centre, and a laser pointed at that mirror throws its reflection onto a screen in a dark room. Play a tone, the balloon moves, and the dot of light begins tracing a shape.

What we found

At most tones the shape stays small. At certain ones it opens right up — the balloon and the tone are moving in step. Eight of those tones sit between 78 and 202 Hz. At 155 Hz the light draws a clean circle; at 150 Hz, a tilted oval. Those are the shapes two crossing waves are known to make, which is how a picture on a screen can tell you what the sound was doing.

KindQuest Technical Inspector

Core Modules, Architecture & Study Results

Communication

A dialogue system through which the player has to understand and work with Ali, rather than around him.

Environmental Accommodation

Players modify barriers in the environment so that Ali can succeed at the task in front of him.

Person-Driven Representation

Ali's behaviour is driven by a probabilistic model informed by real-world Down Syndrome datasets.

The Empathy Journey — narrative spine Empathy meter governs the ending

Ali's performance in the closing task is influenced by the empathy the player has shown, so the ending is earned rather than awarded.

Grounding

Reviewed under the advice of an expert with experience in catering to students with Down Syndrome, and developed in partnership with KDSP. Every interaction parameter traces to peer-reviewed literature rather than to stereotype.

Conclusion

Kind-Quest successfully shifts neurotypical players toward more empathetic and inclusive attitudes, with participants showing better awareness of communication challenges and a realisation for societal inclusion. Further development of the adaptive NPC system and a larger-scale evaluation are the primary next steps.
